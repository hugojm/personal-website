Fixture list

Five days. Any resemblance to a realistic plan is a coincidence.

  1. Wed7 Oct

    Arrival and first diplomatic incident

    We land. The Romans say they are "five minutes away" from their sofa. Dinner is at 20:00, which the Spanish delegation logs as a late lunch. First gelato, declared better than anything in Hannover, where the benchmark was a Lidl freezer.

  2. Thu8 Oct

    Vatican: we ask the Pope one question

    Barça or Madrid. He has to pick. In the Sistine Chapel José Luis shushes people louder than the guards. The Romans confess they have never been inside, like every Roman in history. Trevi at night: one coin to come back, one to apologise.

  3. Fri9 Oct

    Matchday: Barcelona vs Madrid, in the Colosseum 12:30

    The Clásico moves to a neutral ground with no roof and no VAR. Hugo and José Luis enter the arena. The Romans decide with their thumbs. Loser pays the carbonara. Winner also pays the carbonara, because the Romans chose the restaurant.

  4. Sat10 Oct

    Cultural exchange, also known as revenge

    We cook paella for the Italians. They watch in silence, then give a three hour lecture on pasta water. Siesta on the Spanish Steps, which are ours, it says so in the name. Aperitivo becomes dinner becomes "la última", which it is not.

  5. Sun11 Oct

    Trastevere, tears and excess baggage

    Final supplì. Emotional goodbye with hand gestures we still do not understand. We fly home four kilos heavier each. The Romans finally arrive for Thursday's plan.
